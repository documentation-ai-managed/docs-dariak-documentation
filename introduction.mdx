With its modern interface and flexible features, you can focus on delivering clear, comprehensive documentation that engages your users.

## Core Features

Dariak's platform includes robust features designed to enhance your documentation experience. The intuitive editor supports multiple formats, including Markdown and rich text, allowing you to create content that looks professional and is easy to maintain. Real-time collaboration enables teams to work together efficiently, with version control that tracks changes and prevents data loss.

## Best Practices

To get the most out of Dariak, follow these best practices for creating effective documentation. Start with a clear outline that defines your audience and objectives. Use consistent formatting and terminology throughout your documents. Regularly review and update content to keep it current and accurate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan Your Content" icon="target">
    Define your audience and documentation goals before writing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Clear Structure" icon="layers">
    Organize content with headings, sections, and logical flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and Iterate" icon="refresh-cw">
    Review documents for clarity and make improvements based on feedback.
  </Step>
</Steps>
